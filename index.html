About the company

Built around
real problems.

Current & Craft, LLC is an independent software company registered in Florida. We create practical engineering tools, family apps, and interactive learning experiences.

Our work combines engineering experience, thoughtful design, and ongoing improvement through user feedback. Each product starts with a specific need and grows through careful iteration.

The experience behind the work

An engineer’s mindset.
A maker’s curiosity.

Current & Craft was founded by a Florida Professional Engineer with a master’s degree in environmental engineering and experience in civil engineering, construction management, and public infrastructure.

That background brings a practical approach to software: understand the problem, make the important details clear, and build something people can use.

Beyond engineering, a love of making things and the everyday experience of raising a family inspire a different kind of product—tools that simplify home life and games that encourage children to explore.
