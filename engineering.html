Stormwater routing & permitting

StormRoute

Released · Free to use

Hydraulic modeling software developed specifically for projects in South Florida and within the South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD). Model stormwater systems, evaluate storage and exfiltration, and compare existing and proposed conditions.

  • Water-quality credits, trenches, and pond storage
  • Design comparisons and flow/stage hydrographs
  • QA findings and generated permit narratives

Horizontal directional drilling

Reamwright

Available for pilot use

Evaluate bore geometry, pipe loads, and drilling pressures in an HDD workflow, with HDPE as the primary mode and simplified steel-pipe checks.

  • Path radii, tolerances, and as-built comparisons
  • Pull loads, external loads, and collapse checks
  • Hydrofracture analysis and printable reports
