Website Privacy Notice

Effective upon publication: October 29, 2026. Website operated by Current & Craft, LLC.

This notice covers the public marketing pages at currentandcraft.net and www.currentandcraft.net. It does not cover separately hosted applications, such as CommonSlate, which have their own notices.

Information processed when you visit

The website hosting provider may process ordinary technical connection information (for example, IP address, browser details and request times) to serve the website, maintain security and troubleshoot errors. We do not request account sign-in or collect family data through these public marketing pages.

Website usage analytics

This public business website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) for aggregate traffic and product-interest reports. The tag loads without a banner or popup unless you have disabled analytics using the controls below. Google may process device/browser information, approximate network location, and pseudonymous first-party cookie identifiers (including _ga and _ga_*). A functional preference cookie (cc_analytics_preference) remembers your website analytics on/off choice across currentandcraft.net and www.currentandcraft.net.

We measure marketing-page views, which software cards are visible as visitors scroll, clicks on software launch buttons, which section or placement those buttons appeared in, menu/category navigation, and broad page-scroll milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 90%). A launch-button click means a visitor tried to open a product, not that the separately hosted application loaded or was used.

Events use product names/categories, page paths without query strings, referring website origins without referral paths or query strings, and destination website domains without destination paths or query strings. We do not send personal names, account credentials, family or children's data, engineering inputs, uploaded files, form contents, email addresses, or purchase records as custom analytics event data. The company website does not instrument the products themselves.

We do not enable Google Signals, advertising personalization, advertising cookie storage, or retargeting in our website tag. We do not sell website analytics. We use aggregate reports to understand website visits, product interest, and navigation. Google Analytics can still process identifiers and other personal data; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Website analytics preferences

There is no banner or popup. Public website usage analytics is on by default unless your browser restricts it or you switch it off here. You can opt out at any time using the button below. Opting out prevents future analytics transmission in this browser and attempts to remove GA cookies; it does not delete previously processed Google data. Where local law requires prior consent to analytics cookies, an opt-out notice alone is not sufficient and additional regional safeguards are necessary.

Website usage analytics: on

Sharing and retention

We use our hosting provider to serve the website and Google LLC to process optional analytics. We do not share website analytics with advertisers for personalized targeting. Google may process information according to its Privacy Policy. How long GA4 event data is retained depends on the configured GA4 property settings; retention settings should be reviewed in Google Analytics Admin. Reports accessible to our internal marketing reporting tools summarize traffic, product-interest and launch-click totals, and acquisition categories rather than identifying visitor records.

Other websites and products

Links to other websites and applications are governed by those providers' notices. CommonSlate's authenticated family application is separate from these public pages; its children's and family data must not be sent to website analytics.
