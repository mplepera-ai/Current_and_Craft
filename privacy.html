Website Privacy Notice

Effective upon publication: October 29, 2026. Website operated by Current & Craft, LLC.

This notice covers the public marketing pages at currentandcraft.net and www.currentandcraft.net. It does not cover separately hosted applications, such as CommonSlate, which have their own notices.

Information processed when you visit

The website hosting provider may process ordinary technical connection information (for example, IP address, browser details and request times) to serve the website, maintain security and troubleshoot errors. We do not request account sign-in or collect family data through these public marketing pages.

Basic website traffic analytics

This public business website uses Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) to measure aggregate page visits. The GA4 tag loads without a banner or popup unless you have disabled analytics using the controls below. Google may process browser/device and approximate network location information and pseudonymous first-party cookie identifiers (including _ga and _ga_*). The site only sends basic page views, without personal names, family or application data, form contents, visitor email addresses, full URL query strings or customer purchase records.

We do not enable Google Signals, advertising personalization, advertising cookie storage or retargeting in our website tag. We do not sell website analytics. We use only aggregate visitor counts to understand whether our public pages are receiving traffic. Google Analytics can still process identifiers and other personal data; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Website analytics preferences

There is no banner or popup. Basic public website traffic analytics is on by default unless your browser restricts it or you switch it off here. You can opt out at any time using the button below. Opting out prevents future analytics transmission in this browser and attempts to remove GA cookies; it does not delete previously processed Google data. Where local law requires prior consent to analytics cookies, an opt-out notice alone is not sufficient and additional regional safeguards are necessary.

Basic website traffic analytics: on

Sharing and retention

We use our hosting provider to serve the website and Google LLC to process optional analytics. We do not share website analytics with advertisers for personalized targeting. Google may process information according to its Privacy Policy. How long GA4 event data is retained depends on the configured GA4 property settings; retention settings should be reviewed in Google Analytics Admin. Aggregated reports accessible to our internal marketing reporting tools are limited to traffic totals and acquisition categories, not visitor-level records.

Other websites and products

Links to other websites and applications are governed by those providers' notices. CommonSlate's authenticated family application is separate from these public pages; its children's and family data must not be sent to website analytics.

Contact and updates

For privacy inquiries, contact info@currentandcraft.net. We will update this notice if our data practices materially change. Before publishing this notice, the website owner must verify that this contact mailbox is operational.
